## Dates and limits

- Each YAML intake includes a review date, checked official source, applicant-category conditions and all eight admission stages. Unverified stages have a null deadline.
- Where the 2027 intake month is not confirmed, it is left unknown. Real programme availability does not guarantee a 2027 place.
- Alternative matching days are kept as instructions, not represented as a continuous deadline window. Offer-relative acceptance instructions remain relative.
- Maastricht Economics uses its programme-specific September 2027 page. Its 15 January early essay date is not substituted for the 15 March ultimate deadline; general faculty deadline summaries are not used to override it.
- Recommended payment dates and teaching start dates are not converted into mandatory deposit or enrolment cutoffs.
- The documents stage can show the initial paperwork date; its wording preserves later final-diploma dates. Inspect the stage details before treating it as the only document requirement.
- Netherlands calendar deadlines use Europe/Amsterdam for the upcoming seven-day display, including daylight saving. Singapore uses Asia/Singapore. Exact time-of-day cutoffs remain in the wording; the dashboard tracks calendar days.
- Singapore applicant matching is unchanged and its PR form is hidden for Netherlands programmes. Netherlands categories must be selected using the published qualification, citizenship and visa/residence conditions. No automatic Indian-board equivalence is claimed.
- Reader/writer controls remain deferred. No university, housing, scholarship or external data APIs have been added.
